Who we are

Simplifying defence procurement worldwide

Defencetenders is the leading intelligence platform for defence and security procurement. We aggregate, verify, and deliver tender opportunities from government and military sources across 50+ countries.

Our platform empowers defence suppliers, contractors, and consultants to discover relevant opportunities faster, track procurement activity, and win more contracts in the global defence market.
